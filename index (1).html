About NextPlay

More than a highlight reel.

NextPlay was created as a student website project around a simple idea: athletes should have an easy, organized way to show college coaches what makes them stand out.

Recruiting can feel overwhelming. A personal athlete page can make it easier to put game footage, achievements, and background information together without sending coaches a dozen different links.

Our goal is to make athletic stories easier to find, understand, and share.
