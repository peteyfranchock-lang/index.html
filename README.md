NextPlay – Athlete Recruitment Website

About NextPlay

NextPlay is a website designed to help student-athletes showcase their skills, highlight videos, and achievements to college coaches. The goal is to make the recruiting process easier by giving athletes a place to share their athletic information.

Website Features

Home: Introduces NextPlay and its purpose.

Athlete Highlights: Showcases athletes and their athletic achievements.

About: Explains how NextPlay helps athletes get recruited.

Contact: Allows coaches and visitors to find contact information.

Purpose

I created NextPlay because I am interested in sports and wanted to design something that could help student-athletes gain more exposure during the college recruiting process.
